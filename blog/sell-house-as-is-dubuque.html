Can You Sell a House As-Is in Dubuque?

If you’ve recently inherited a house, entered into pre-foreclosure, or have a property you no longer want, you may be looking for ways to sell it quickly. While the housing market in Dubuque is fairly solid, it can still take between 3 and 6 months or more for a traditional sale to go through. If your house is in need of repairs, cleaning, or updating, this timetable can increase drastically. Not to mention the high cost of getting it ready for the market, realtor fees, and more. If you want to sell house as is Dubuque residents are in luck. The county allows you to bypass the time-consuming and costly approach of selling your house through a realtor, for the faster option of selling “as-is”. In this article, we’ll explain what it means to sell your house as-is, how the process works, and why it could be the best choice for your situation.

What Does Selling “As-Is” Mean?

Selling a house as-is in Dubuque means offering the property in its current condition, without cleaning or making renovations or repairs before closing. The buyer accepts full responsibility for the house with all existing faults, and the seller is not responsible for providing financial compensation for any defects. What are some important factors to consider with an as-is sale?

You don’t have to work on the property - As the seller, you’re not responsible for any repairs, including broken HVAC units, leaky roofs, or cosmetic issues.

You must still disclose defects - While you’re not responsible for fixing them, under Iowa law, you must still disclose any known defects to the home, including asbestos, mold, and structural issues.

You may still need to get an inspection - Depending on the buyer, you may still need to get an information-only inspection of the property so they know what to expect, even if you aren’t required to make repairs.

You may get less than a traditional sale - One of the reasons as-is sales are so popular is that they’re generally much faster than traditional sales; however, you may get less for the property.

Is it Legal to Sell a House As Is in Dubuque?

Yes, in Dubuque, it is legal to sell a house as-is as long as you fill out an Iowa Residential Property Seller Disclosure Statement disclosing all known issues. It’s crucial that you don’t hide anything from the buyer, as this could result in legal issues down the line. Most buyers and investors who purchase as-is properties understand the inherent risk that comes with these houses, so as long as you’re open and honest and do your due diligence, it should be a smooth and positive experience.

Why Would You Sell Your House As-Is?

Selling a house as-is is a much different experience than listing on the traditional market. There’s numerous reasons why you may choose to sell your property this way, including the following.

You Have a Financial Need

Making repairs on a house can get expensive quickly, and many people simply can’t afford to do them. Instead of pouring thousands of dollars into the property, selling as-is lets you skip this step and save your hard-earned money.

It’s Fast and Convenient

When you use a reputable company, selling your house as-is for cash is fast and convenient. You don’t have to worry about the hassle of hiring contractors to make repairs, spending hours cleaning, or working with a realtor who requires a large fee for their services. Instead of waiting months, the sale is complete in only a few weeks.

You Inherited a Property

If you’ve unexpectedly inherited a house that you don’t want to live in, you know it can be more of a hassle than a blessing. If you don’t want to take on the expense and responsibility, selling it as-is may be a good option.

You’re Entering Pre-Foreclosure

If you’ve fallen behind on your mortgage payments and want to avoid foreclosure, you could sell your house quickly as-is. Selling your house before the foreclosure process begins helps protect your credit and may leave you with more options down the line.

The Downsides of Selling a House As-Is in Dubuque

While there’s many benefits to selling your house as-is, it’s important to consider the possible downsides as well.

The sale price may be lower - As-is cash sales may be lower than traditional sales. However, a reputable company will still provide fair market value, so be sure to do your research beforehand.

The buyer pool can be smaller - Lenders have strict safety standards when approving loans for as-is houses, so the buyer pool may be lower than traditional sales.

Perception of risk - Listing a home as-is can lead buyers to assume there are hidden issues with the property.
